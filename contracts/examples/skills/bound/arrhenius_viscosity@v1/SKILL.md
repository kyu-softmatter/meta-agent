# arrhenius_viscosity

**Gives:** a viscosity at the wanted temperature. It is a correlation, and the row it gives says so.

**Needs:** four rows of the sheet: the reference viscosity, the temperature it holds at, the liquid's activation temperature, and the wanted temperature. The row is graded by the worst of them.

**When it applies:** a simple liquid, a modest temperature span. For water over a wide span a fitted correlation from its own source is another skill.

**What it does not say:** anything about a mixture or a non-Newtonian fluid.

Run it through the runner; never type its result.
