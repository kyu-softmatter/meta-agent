# literature_span

**Gives:** the lowest and the highest of the handed values and range edges, which material gave each, how many values and how many stated ranges were handed, or why there is no span.

**Needs:** one entry for each value or stated error range of the quantity, all in one unit. Each entry names the material it was read from, says in words how that material's system, quantity and engine compare with the task's, and gives either one value, with the uncertainty its material states for it where it states one, or the low and the high edge of one range. Where an uncertainty is given, the entry also says what kind the material states.

**When it applies:** any quantity of any engine or instrument whose reference states no uncertainty and for which the person gave no tolerance. It comes after the reference's stated uncertainty and before the last printed digit and the run's own error.

**What it never does:** widen the span by a statistic or a multiplier, or by an uncertainty its material does not state, take a value whose material or comparison is not written, judge whether a material is suitable, find or read a material, judge a result, or overturn a pass the person gave.

Run it through the runner; never type its result.
