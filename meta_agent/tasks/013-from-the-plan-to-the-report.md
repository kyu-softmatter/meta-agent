**Seat:** the four contracts, bundle check B13 and their fixtures are the design seat's and land first. The template's gate and runners, the subagent definitions' use of them and the mock run are a `meta-…` seat's.

**Goal:** an agreed range becomes a plan card; `operator` runs it within its intervals through the gate; `analyst` analyses the data; a report goes to the user. On a mock the flow runs from its first card to its last without the Meta-Agent, and every hand-over is a card that a check can judge.

**What is settled (the person, 2026-11-09):** the negotiation produces a card for carrying the experiment out; an operating subagent runs the experiment within the range with the skills; an analysing subagent analyses the data with the data-analysis skills; a report is written and reported to the user; the names and fields of the four cards are the seat's to decide; under adoption as agreed a run on hardware starts without a person being asked (plan.md 14-33(i), 14-34(d)). Standing: safety values are the person's (MP4), a `write_device` skill is released from `held` by a person, the gate runs before every call, and the Meta-Agent never operates (MP1).

**Method (the names and fields are the seat's by the person's word; the rest recommended, not settled):**
- Design seat, four contracts in the template beside `tool_contract`:
  - `run_plan`: `range_sheet_ref`; `adoption` (the value it was made under); `negotiation {mode: skipped | held, reason, rounds, positions[] {subagent, kind, hard[], soft[], concession_order[]}, proposals[] {round, configuration, relaxes[], reason}, answers[] {round, subagent, hard: pass | fail | not_set, soft: accept | object, reason}, outcome: agreed | reported_to_person}`; `configuration`; `parameters[]`, each `{name, interval {min, max, unit, min_from, max_from}, start (a computed value with `basis` and the `choice` behind it), adjustable, safety_relevant}`; `steps[]`, each `{n, skill, inputs, expects}`; `stop_conditions[]`; `data {saves, where}`; `authorisation`, `null` or, under `confirm`, `{plan_hash, approved_by, author: human}` or, under `adopt`, `{by_policy: adopt, policy_revision}`; `status`.
  - `run_record`: `plan_ref`, `plan_hash`; `environment: none | mock | bench`; `gate[]`, each check in order with its verdict; `calls[]`, each `{n, step, skill, tool, verdict, error}`; `readings[]`, numbers with `measured:<run id>`; `adjustments[]`, each `{parameter, from, to, on_reading}`, every one inside its interval; `person_steps[]`, recorded as `operator_read`; `departures[]`, each `{at_step, why, state}`; `data[] {path, sha256, bytes}`; `ended: completed | stopped | refused`.
  - `analysis`: `run_ref`; `data[]` by hash; `results[]`, `computed` numbers whose `basis` names the skill, its version and the data's hash; `quality[]`, each `{dimension, planned, obtained, verdict: met | not_met | not_assessed, reason}` over the dimensions of plan.md 14-35; `question {answered: yes | partly | no, by: objective, reason}`; `gaps[]`.
  - `report`: `question`; `answer`; `numbers[]`; `unknown[]`; `next_condition {proposed_by: objective, what, why}`; `refs {range_sheet, run_plan, run_record, analysis}`; an MD beside the JSON, every number of the MD in a card.
- Design seat, B13: the four contracts are in the bundle; fixture cards honour them; an `adjustments[]` item outside its interval, a `run_record` whose `plan_hash` differs from its plan's, a `results[]` number with no `basis`, a `report` number absent from the cards, a `run_plan` for a `write_device` step with no place for `authorisation`, and an `authorisation` by policy where `envelope/policy.json` is unset or not set by a person each fail. Rejected fixtures, one per assertion.
- Meta seat, the gate: `src/gate.py` gains, after the safety check it has, `authorise` (the plan exists; for a plan with a `write_device` step, under `confirm` its hash equals the signed one, and under `adopt` the policy is set by a person and the gate itself has the limit check and `inside`, otherwise it behaves as `confirm`), the run lock, and `inside` (a call's inputs lie in the plan's intervals). The limits themselves stay the destination's (S7); in this repository the signature and the policy are fixtures and the device a mock.
- Meta seat, `src/operate.py`: runs `steps[]` through `run_tool.py` in order, lets `operator` adjust an `adjustable` parameter inside its interval on a `hardware_state` reading, stops where a step would leave an interval or a verification fails and writes `departures[]`, and writes the `run_record`. `src/analyse.py` runs the analysis skills on the data by hash and writes `analysis`; `src/report.py` writes `report` from the cards.
- Meta seat, subagents: `operator` (every hardware kind; `010`'s `reader` folded into it) and `analyst`; `objective` answers `analysis.question` and proposes `report.next_condition`. The agent's seat writes the cards; a subagent writes none.
- Meta seat, the mock flow: on the fixture bundle of `012`, a request runs `range_sheet` → `run_plan` → `run_record` → `analysis` → `report` on the recorded transcript, with fixture answers where a model would speak, so that two runs write identical cards.
- Tests: the flow completes on the mock and its `report`'s numbers are all in cards; under `confirm` with the signature missing the gate refuses before any tool call; under `adopt` set by a person the same plan runs with no signature and the record names the policy revision; under `adopt` with the policy unset, or set by a seat, it refuses; a call outside an interval is refused by `inside` and the record ends `stopped`; a reading that asks for more than the interval allows produces a departure and no adjustment; an adjustment inside the interval is recorded with its reading; the `analysis` of the fixture data recomputes from its `basis`; `objective`'s answer `no` puts a next condition in the report.
