**Method (recommended, not settled):**
- Design seat, schema: a row gains `bounds[]`, each `{side: min | max, value, unit, source: computed:<skill id@vN>, grade, value_kind: computed, basis: {skill, inputs: {<input name>: <row name>}}, uncertainty}`, and `interval {min, max, unit, min_from, max_from}`; the sheet gains `skills_considered[]`, each `{skill, verdict: used | not_applied, why}`, and `objectives[]`; a row's `recommendation {mode: interval | neighbourhood | tradeoff | lookup | scale | gap, basis, candidates, frontier, chosen_by}` as in 14-26. `grades.json` gains the row `computed → max(E4, worst input)`, the flat row kept under `retired_on`.
- Design seat, checks: check 4 (a `safety_relevant: true` row has `value: null` or `author: human`, and no `recommendation` with a point). Check 19 (recompute and coverage): every `bounds[]` item is recomputed by running the skill and version its `basis` names on the values of the input rows it names, in the manifest's units, and must agree to the stated `uncertainty` or, without one, to its significant figures; its grade is the worst of E4 and the input rows' grades; `interval.min` is the largest `min` bound and `interval.max` the smallest `max` bound, and `min_from`, `max_from` name them; an interval with `min > max` carries no point and a `recommendation` of `tradeoff` or `gap`; every skill of kind `bound` whose `applies_to` matches the row's parameter and the sheet's tool is in `skills_considered[]`, and a `not_applied` has a `why`; a `computed` row or bound without `basis` fails. Rejected fixtures: a bound whose value the skill does not give; a bound graded better than an input; an interval wider than its bounds; a point on an empty interval; an applicable skill left out; a `not_applied` with no reason; a safety row with a bound used as its value.
- Meta seat, the bound skills (`skills/bound/<id>@v1/`, each with a worked case from the source it cites, on the fixture profiles until 14-20 and 14-21 name the engine and the rheometer). For a rotational rheometer: `shear_rate_max_from_max_speed`, `shear_rate_min_from_min_speed` (geometry factor times the angular velocity limits); `shear_stress_min_from_min_torque`, `shear_stress_max_from_max_torque`; `viscosity_min_measurable_at_rate` (minimum torque over the rate); `shear_rate_max_from_inertia` (the secondary-flow criterion for the geometry); `gap_min_from_particle_size`. For a continuum engine: `timestep_max_from_courant`, `timestep_max_from_diffusion`, `timestep_max_from_relaxation_time`. A criterion constant (the Courant limit, the multiple of the particle diameter, the fraction of the relaxation time, the critical number of the inertia criterion) is an input, bound to a row that has its own source: `literature:` when a source states it, `assumed:` at E5 otherwise, so the bound's grade says what it rests on and the person can replace the row. Dimensionless numbers (Re, Wi, De, Pe, Ca) are plain functions in a sibling module the skills import, not skills of their own, since they bound nothing.
- Meta seat, the pass (`src/parameters/bounds.py`): for a parameter of the goal, `catalogue` lists the applicable bound skills with their descriptions and inputs; the model, in the session, writes `skills_considered[]` (which it uses, which it does not and why) and for each used skill the binding of inputs to rows; `python -m meta_agent.src.parameters.bounds apply <sheet draft>` runs each skill through `010`'s runner, propagates first-order uncertainty from the input rows, rounds to the worst input's significant figures, writes `bounds[]` and `interval`, and stops with a named error when a binding points at a row that is missing, has no value, or has the wrong unit. The model's binding is a file, so the same file gives the same sheet; the model never writes a bound's value.
- Meta seat, the point (`src/parameters/point.py`): the rules are code, and this task applies each where its condition holds; choosing among them is the model's from `011` on, recorded with its reason (plan.md 14-32(a)). The rules: with one objective and a non-empty interval, the log-midpoint, named in `basis` (`interval`); with an anchor row and `near:<row>` in the goal, a scan of ± one decade in half-decade steps kept inside the interval, as candidates, and a `gap` whose `requires` names the measurement or calibration that would pin the value (`neighbourhood`); with two objectives pulling one parameter, or an empty interval, the two bounds that collide and, where the objectives can be evaluated, the Pareto set on a log grid with its knee, the row `UNDECIDED` and `chosen_by: human` (`tradeoff`); whether such a row waits for a person is the adoption setting's from `012` on (plan.md 14-32(j)), and this task leaves it `UNDECIDED`. `lookup` and `scale` are as in 14-26, a `scale` correlation being a skill with `side: value`.
