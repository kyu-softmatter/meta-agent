# 011 — Rough ranges of the key quantities, and the model's choice

**Status:** open, 2026-11-09. Opened by `design-macbook-20261109-1` on the person's words (`../../docs/ideas/20261109.md`: "각각의 파라미터들을 스킬로 구할 수 있으면 좋겠고, 그렇게 얻은 값들을 LLM이 적절히 리즈닝 해 엔진 파라미터를 정하면 좋겠음", "핵심 물리량들의 대략적인 범위를 찾을 수 있으면 좋겠다", "대략적 범위 단계를 별도 카드로 둔다"), recorded in plan.md 14-32. Nothing exists yet. Order: `010` → `006` → `007` → this → `012` → `013`; it needs `007` (bound skills, the runner's use, the sheet's `bounds[]`).

**Seat:** the `range_sheet` schema, the two new lines of the kinds vocabulary, the configuration axes on `tool_profile`, check 23 and their fixtures are the design seat's and land first. The pass, the skills and the fixture microscope are a `meta-…` seat's.

**Goal:** before any precise value, the rough range of every key quantity is on a card, found by considering the limits together. Skills give every number; code lists the tool's configurations and keeps the ones that meet the requirements; the model chooses (which skills, which quantity first, which rule or candidate) and says why, and types no number.

**What is settled (the person, 2026-11-09):** each key quantity is obtained by a skill; the limits are considered together; rough ranges are what is wanted; the model reasons over the skills' values and sets the engine parameters; the pass is a card of its own. Added 2026-11-12 (`../../docs/ideas/20261112.md`: "근사치 추정과 파라미터 계산을 위한 파이썬(deterministic) 계산도 각각 스킬로 만들면 좋을것 같네"): the deterministic Python calculations for approximate estimates and for parameter calculation are each made a skill; how the design seat reads it is in plan.md 14-32, as recommendations. Standing: a model's number is E6 and enters nothing; a safety-relevant row gets a value from nothing (check 4).

**Method (recommended, not settled):**
- Design seat, the card: `contracts/schemas/range_sheet.schema.json`, copied into the template so that the Meta-Agent's and a bundle's are one card. Fields beside the common ones: `tool_profile_ref`; `scales[]`, each `{quantity, min, max}` where `min` and `max` are numbers with `basis {skill, inputs}`; `requirements[]`, each `{id, quantity, side: min | max, from: <scale>, held_by, hardness: hard | soft}`; `axes[]`, each `{name, options[], claim_ref}`; `configurations[]`, each `{id, choice {<axis>: <option>}, delivers[] (numbers, `basis` naming a `capability` skill), verdicts[] {requirement, met}}`; `feasible[]`; `ranges[]`, each `{quantity, min, max}` over the feasible configurations; `collisions[]`, the pairs of requirements no configuration meets together; `skills_considered[]` as on the sheet; `choices[]`, each `{what: skill | order | rule | candidate, by: model, chosen, reason}`.
- Design seat, vocabulary and profile: the kinds of `010` gain `scale` and `capability`; a `tool_profile` claim may carry `options[]`, the discrete settings of one axis, under the rules every claim has (check 11).
- Design seat, check 23: every number of `scales[]` and `delivers[]` is recomputed from the skill its `basis` names; `verdicts[]` follow from the numbers; `feasible[]` is exactly the configurations that meet every hard requirement; `ranges[]` are the least and the greatest over `feasible[]`; `configurations[]` is the full product of `axes[]`, none left out; an axis names a claim, and one that is `inferred` or `unknown` is marked so; an empty `feasible[]` has `collisions[]` and no range; a `choices[]` item has a reason and names a rule or a candidate that exists; a number typed with no `basis` fails. Rejected fixtures, one per assertion.
- Meta seat, the pass (`src/parameters/ranges.py`): `python -m meta_agent.src.parameters.ranges build <draft>` reads the model's choices file (which scale and capability skills, the bindings, the order), runs the skills through `010`'s runner, lists the configurations, fills verdicts, `feasible[]`, `ranges[]` and `collisions[]`. When a row is set, what depends on it is recomputed. No optimiser and no solver.
- Meta seat, the first fixture and skills, written by hand from the person's example and from no device: a fixture microscope profile (a camera with its full-frame rate and its rate against rows read, objectives, binning modes, regions of interest); scale skills (a diffusion time from size and viscosity, an advection time from a speed and a length); capability skills (the sample-plane pixel, the field of view, the highest frame rate of a configuration, the signal per frame); the bounds they feed (frame interval from the fastest timescale, exposure from motion blur, recording length from the slowest timescale, the pixel from the optical resolution). A judgement constant (a sampling factor, a multiple of the slowest time) is an input row with its own source, as in `007`.
- Tests: the fixture request whose dynamics fill the frame keeps only full-frame configurations and its frame-rate range is capped by them; the same request with a loose pixel requirement keeps lower magnifications and binned modes and its frame-rate range rises; a request no configuration meets gives `collisions[]` naming two requirements and no range; two runs write identical cards; a choices file that names a missing rule is refused.

**Done when (read off a run):**
- `python contracts/validate.py --expect-fail` rejects every fixture under `check23_*`; `python contracts/validate.py` prints `0 failed`.
- The pass on the fixture microscope request writes a `range_sheet` with the two cases above as two requests, and a second run writes identical cards.
- `PYTHONUTF8=1 python -m unittest discover -s meta_agent/tests -t .` is OK.

**Must not:** let the model write a number; put a judgement constant inside a skill; add an optimiser or a solver; leave a configuration out of the listing; write a value on a safety-relevant row; treat an `inferred` axis as observed; write outside `meta_agent/` (meta seat) or inside `meta_agent/src/` and `meta_agent/skills/` (design seat).

**Report:** ten lines or fewer — the skills by kind and their case results, the fixture count of check 23, the feasible configurations and the frame-rate range of each of the two cases, what the impossible request produced, the verdict line, the test count.
