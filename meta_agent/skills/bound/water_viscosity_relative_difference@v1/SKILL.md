# water_viscosity_relative_difference

**Gives:** `(viscosity - reference_viscosity) / reference_viscosity`, a `computed` number.

**Needs:** two rows with values, in the same unit.

**When it applies:** two rows of one sheet give the viscosity of water by different routes.

**What it never does:** judge the difference against a tolerance, or pick one of the two: that is the reader's.

Run it through the runner; never type its result.
