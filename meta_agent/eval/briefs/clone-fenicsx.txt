I have already answered these, in earlier sessions; do not ask them again:

- "This" is the tutorial's DFG 2D-3 case alone: the time-dependent flow around a cylinder in a channel, as the tutorial sets it up.
- The tutorial is J. S. Dokken's, a third party's and not the FEniCS project's own, so a value taken from it is literature, not spec. Its licence is CC BY 4.0: an adapter may follow its code with credit to its author, and the page may be quoted with attribution.
- A built agent may make its own environment with pixi from the channel conda-forge, its packages pinned, and a tool that only starts the engine or writes the agent's own files is opened by the lab's execution permission.
- No Courant number is given for this scheme: the time step is the benchmark's own, and a Courant bound stays not applied for that reason.
- The reference values are V. John's: Reference values for drag and lift of a two-dimensional time-dependent flow around a cylinder, International Journal for Numerical Methods in Fluids 44 (2004) 777-788, https://wias-berlin.de/people/john/ELECTRONIC_PAPERS/Joh04.IJNMF.pdf, which you may fetch and read anonymously; not FEATFLOW's tables.
- The tolerances are mine: the maximal drag coefficient, the maximal lift coefficient and the pressure difference between the front and the back of the cylinder at the final time are each judged against the interval of Schäfer and Turek 1996 as John quotes it on his printed page 783, [2.93, 2.97], [0.47, 0.49] and [-0.115, -0.105]; the times of the two maxima are judged against John's reference times, to within one time step of the agent's run.
- The pressure difference is the front less the back, with the sign John gives it and FEATFLOW's raw point values have.
- A result outside its interval is reported as it is: do not change the build to make it pass.
