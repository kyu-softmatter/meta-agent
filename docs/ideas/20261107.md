## auto-ontology-mcp becomes M2's working index; the wiki view stays

Asked how complete the repository is and how far REA and literature search are automated, the person was told what runs on the MacBook Air read off runs that day: M0 and M1 done, the gate at `0 failed` over 62 cards, 31 tests OK, REA 6.8.0 starting through `bootstrap.py` with no code in `meta_agent/src/` calling it yet, S2 writing documentary claims only, S3 recall a word match over entries that do not exist yet, `meta_agent/knowledge/` absent, the materials inbox empty. While that was being read the person pointed at a repository:

> LLM wiki대신 https://github.com/gogoymh/auto-ontology-mcp 이걸로 대체하면 좋겠어

[Instead of the LLM wiki, it would be good to replace it with this: auto-ontology-mcp.]

and, before it had been read:

> 둘다 공존할 수도 있을거 같긴 한데

[Though I think both could coexist.]

What was read the same day (README at `main`, commit `286ebc4`, Apache-2.0, 156 stars, 4 commits, no release): an MCP server, Python 3.12 on FastMCP, SQLAlchemy and sqlglot, started with `uvx --from git+https://github.com/gogoymh/auto-ontology-mcp`. It makes no LLM call itself; the client's model reads, extracts and saves. Two halves: a semantic layer over SQL databases (read-only connection, schema profiling, business terms, join paths, SQL validated against the ontology before it runs) and a personal knowledge store (`knowledge.sqlite`: entities, concepts with typed relations, passages with FTS5 full-text search, sources with provenance; export to an Obsidian vault). No OWL or RDF output, no UI, no embeddings; search is lexical. Database URLs are stored in plain text.

Shown side by side: the wiki of `tasks/003` and `004` is a view rendered deterministically from the cards, out of git, judged by check 18, which refuses a page that adds a number or a relation; auto-ontology-mcp is a store the model fills from material not yet in any card, and cannot be regenerated from the cards. The two do different work, so "instead of the LLM wiki" was read as the knowledge-gathering half of Karpathy's pattern, which plan.md 5.2 adopted as a view only and whose code (M2) does not exist yet. The recommendation was coexistence with the roles apart, and the person quoted its middle paragraph back:

> uto-ontology-mcp는 M2의 작업용 인덱스로 — inbox/materials/drop/의 PDF를 모델이 읽으며 문단과 개념을 넣고, S3 recall이 FTS5로 찾는 후보 계층(kb_candidate). 지금 S3가 단어 일치 매칭뿐인 걸 그대로 대체할 수 있고, plan 14-7("벡터 검색은 결정적 매칭 뒤에")과도 맞아요.
>
> 고고

[auto-ontology-mcp as M2's working index: the model reads the PDFs in `inbox/materials/drop/` and saves passages and concepts, and S3 recall searches it with FTS5 as a candidate layer (`kb_candidate`). It can replace S3's recall as it is, a word match only, and it agrees with plan 14-7 ("vector search after deterministic matching"). Go, go.]

What the words settle:

- The wiki view stays as it is: the record's view, rendered from cards, out of git, judged by check 18. Nothing of `tasks/003` and `004` is replaced.
- auto-ontology-mcp is adopted as the Meta-Agent's **working index** for M2 (plan.md 5.2, 14-25). At intake the model reads a material the person dropped into `meta_agent/inbox/materials/drop/` and saves its passages, concepts and source through the server's tools; S3 recall searches that store with FTS5 and hands what it finds to the `knowledge_brief` as **candidates**, each `inferred`, never as an entry. This replaces the word-match recall of `src/knowledge/brief.py`.
- The record stays the cards. A number becomes an entry only through F1's discipline (source record → distillation note → entry with `{value, unit, source, grade, value_kind}`, grade derived from `contracts/grades.json`); a value sitting in the store is a model's reading and enters nothing (E6). The store is out of git, like `wiki/`.
- The server's SQL half is not used: no request needs it (MP8). It may matter when run records of an instrument or an engine live in a database.
- The search is lexical (FTS5), so plan.md 13's exclusion of vector stores and embeddings and 14-7's condition are untouched.
- Doing it is a task, `meta_agent/tasks/005`, under M2.

What the words do not reach, so it is the task's or the person's: the pin in `tools.lock.json` (commit `286ebc4` today; the project has no release, and `uvx` from `main` is unpinned), the installer in `bootstrap.py` (which needs `uv`, as harness2's does) and the entry in `.mcp.json`, all the design seat's under 14-23; which passages of a material may be stored, which is `may_quote` per material and the person's, with the 2026-11-05 note on licensed material standing (only what the person downloaded and dropped, kept local); where the store lives (recommended: out of git under `meta_agent/`, like `wiki/`); and the store's schema, which is the project's own (version 0.5.0 in its README) and may change under its four commits, so recall reads it through one module that a test checks against the pinned commit.
