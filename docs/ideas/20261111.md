## The testbed tests; it does not shape the development

After the design seat read the repository at `780d424` and showed how a bundle is built and how the fixture rheometer bundle (`meta_agent/builds/rheo-fixture/0001`) runs, the person said:

> 지금 rheometer와 그 시뮬레이션 엔진을 대상으로 개발하면 안됨. 우리의 테스트 베드일 뿐이라서 지금 미리 그걸 염두하고 개발하면 다른것에 범용 적용이 어려움

[Development must not target the rheometer and its simulation engine now. They are only our testbed, so developing with them in mind in advance makes it hard to apply the result generally to other things.]

What the words settle: the rheometer (testbed B) and the rheology simulation engine (testbed C) are a testbed and not a design target. What is built now (the Designer, the contracts and checks, the bundle template, the skill library and its runner, the subagents, the run from plan to report) is built for scientific instruments and simulation engines in general, and is not shaped in advance by what the rheometer or its engine needs. The decision of 2026-10-29 stands: the first testbed is the rheometer and a rheology engine as a pair, done when a cross-validation card exists. The testbed is where the general machinery is tried, later; it is not what the machinery is written for.

What the words do not settle, and what the design seat found when it read the repository the same day (a term search for rheometry words over the general parts at `780d424`):

- The machinery reads as general by that search: `contracts/validate.py`, `contracts/skill_kinds.json`, the bundle template under `meta_agent/templates/` and the Designer have no rheometry term; `meta_agent/src/onboarding/discover.py` maps the word "rheometer" to the registry's `lab_rheometer` and names RheoTool beside other engines; `contracts/units.json` carries rheology units, as vocabulary. A term search does not show that a structure is general, only that it names no rheometer.
- The content does not: of the 21 skills in `meta_agent/skills/`, 17 apply only to `lab_rheometer` or to the rheology engines (`rheotool`, `openfoam`), and all 6 hardware skills (connection, state, range, boot, control, calibration) are the fixture rheometer's; the other 4 are correlations for water. The fixture request `req-rheo-fixture-001` and its bundle exercise the machinery on a rheometer only.
- Whether that content stays as the testbed's examples, is recast, or is joined by fixtures of other kinds is the person's (plan.md 14-37), as is what the open tasks 011 to 014 are written against.
